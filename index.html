Questions, answered

How do I pay after ordering?

Orders are handled by email — there's no online checkout. Click an Order button and you'll receive a reply with payment options and an invoice (bank transfer details included; other methods on request). Your signed license file and download link arrive by email once payment clears, usually the same or next business day.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Once installed, cameras, live view, motion events and recordings work completely offline. You only need internet to download the installer and updates — and for remote viewing, if you set that up yourself on your network.

Which cameras are supported?

Any IP camera that exposes a standard RTSP stream — which covers most brands (Hikvision, Dahua, TP-Link Tapo, Reolink, Ezviz and many others), plus ONVIF-capable devices. Old analog cameras can be used through any RTSP-capable DVR/NVR.

What happens when the trial or license expires?

The system switches to the free Lite edition: one camera, permanently, with all your data and recordings intact. Activate a license at any time to restore full capacity — nothing is deleted and nothing is bricked.

Can I move my license to a new computer?

Yes. Licenses can be re-issued for a new machine — contact support with your order details and you'll receive a fresh activation file.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a few hundred megabytes of disk space for the app, plus storage for recordings. Any PC that can run a modern browser can run CCTV VMS.

Is there a subscription or per-camera fee?

No. Pricing is one-time per site. The 1-year license includes upgrades and can be renewed — but the Lite edition is free forever and paid licenses can be issued as perpetual.
